## TECHNICAL DETAILS

### Calculation Formula
1. Calculate sheets: `(quantity / items_per_sheet) × 1.05`
2. Stock cost: `(sheets / 1000) × stock_price`
3. Print cost: `sheets × sides × print_rate`
4. Cutting cost: `(sheets / 500) × $11`
5. Setup: `$27 + (artworks - 1) × $15`
6. Subtotal: Sum all costs
7. Apply margin tier (11 tiers based on subtotal)
8. Apply GST: `qty<1000: ×1.1×1.1`, `qty≥1000: 10% discount then ×1.1`
