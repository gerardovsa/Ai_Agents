Features

  • Streaming Transcript: Text appears in chunks for visual effect
  • Voice Control: Choose from available system voices
  • Speed Control: Adjust speech rate (0.5x - 2.0x)
  • Pitch Control: Adjust voice pitch (0 - 2)
  • Volume Control: Adjust speech volume (0% - 100%)
  • Pause/Resume: Pause and resume speech mid-sentence
  • Stop: Stop speech at any time
  • Copy to Clipboard: Copy transcript text easily
  • Auto-scroll: Transcript automatically scrolls to latest content
  • Visual Feedback: Color-coded status indicators (blue=speaking, green=success, red=error, yellow=paused)
