✓ Recommendation

💡 FLUORESCENT Variations (V53-V57) - ULTRA BRIGHT!

These are the brightest possible! Pure cyan/aqua with glow effects (text-shadow + icon drop-shadow):

  • V53 (Neon Cyan #00ffff) - Maximum brightness, pure cyan glow 🔥
  • V56 (Ultra Cyan #00f0ff) - Intense glow, slightly softer than V53
  • V57 (Aqua Glow #00fff5) - Aqua-tinted, very eye-catching
  • V54 (Electric Cyan #00e5ff) - Bright electric blue-cyan
  • V55 (Neon Sky #00d4ff) - Sky blue with neon intensity

⚠️ Note: These fluorescent colors are MUCH brighter than V40, V43, V45. They use text-shadow and drop-shadow for extra glow!

⭐ Prime-Style Blue Variations (V38-V52)

These match the Prime-Loaded style exactly (transparent background + colored border/text/icon). Best options:

  • V44 (GitHub Blue #58a6ff) - Bright, modern, excellent contrast
  • V45 (Info Blue #79c0ff) - Lighter, softer, very readable
  • V41 (Azure #3b82f6) - Classic blue, strong presence
  • V42 (Light Azure #60a5fa) - Balanced brightness
  • V39 (Sky Blue #38bdf8) - Vivid and eye-catching

Other Good Options:

Variation 6 (Medium Blue Solid) or Variation 7 (Lighter Gradient) provide the best readability while maintaining visual hierarchy with Prime-Loaded.

Variation 9 (Dark + Bold) is good if you want more distinction between Prime and Agents.

Variation 12 or 14 (Accent Blue + Border) work well if you want a lighter, outlined style.

Variation 15 (Quick-Nav Style) matches your existing quick-nav badges perfectly!
