How It Works

  • STT (Speech-to-Text):
    • Click microphone button to start recording
    • Speak into your microphone
    • Audio is sent to Whisper backend in 5-second chunks
    • Transcript appears in real-time and inserts into chat input
    • Click microphone again to stop recording
  • TTS (Text-to-Speech):
    • AI responses are automatically spoken
    • Transcript streams into TTS container with visual effects
    • Adjust voice, speed, pitch, and volume as needed
    • Pause/resume/stop controls available
  • Complete Workflow:
    • 1️⃣ Speak your question (STT converts to text)
    • 2️⃣ AI processes and responds
    • 3️⃣ Response is spoken back to you (TTS)
    • 4️⃣ Both transcripts are preserved for reference
