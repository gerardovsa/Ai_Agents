# Xero Quick Prompts - User Guide
**Feature Release:** December 23, 2024  
**Version:** 1.0

---

## 🚀 What is Quick Prompts?

Quick Prompts is a powerful feature that provides **pre-written AI analysis questions** for your Xero dashboards. Instead of manually typing questions to your AI assistant, you can select from expert-designed prompts that automatically include your dashboard data and a complete analytical framework.

---

## 📍 Where to Find It

Look for the **purple "Quick Prompts" button** next to the "Export for AI" button in the top-right corner of any Xero dashboard:

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  XERO DASHBOARD HEADER                   │
│                                          │
│  [🎯 Quick Prompts ▼]  [📋 Export]     │ ← Click here!
└─────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

---

## ⚡ Quick Start (3 Steps)

### Step 1: Click "Quick Prompts"
Click the purple "Quick Prompts" button to open the dropdown menu.

### Step 2: Choose Your Analysis Type
You'll see two types of prompts:

**🎯 STRATEGIC ANALYSIS** (Gold badges)
- These are powerful multi-step frameworks that work for any dashboard
- Examples: Deep Insights Analysis, Actionable Strategy, Historical Comparison

**📊 DASHBOARD-SPECIFIC PROMPTS** (Blue sections)
- Quick questions tailored to the current dashboard
- Examples: "Compare YoY growth trends", "Which business needs attention?"

### Step 3: Paste into AI Chat
After clicking a prompt:
1. Data + prompt are automatically copied to your clipboard ✅
2. A green success message confirms the copy
3. Open your AI assistant (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.)
4. Press **Ctrl+V** to paste
5. Press **Enter** to send

**That's it!** The AI will analyze your data using the selected framework.

## 🎯 The 6 Strategic Prompts

These powerful prompts work for **all dashboards** and provide structured analytical frameworks:

### 1. 🔬 **Deep Insights Analysis**
**Best for:** Discovering hidden patterns and multi-domain insights  
**Time required:** 2-3 minutes  
**What you get:**
- Analysis from 5 business perspectives (Financial, Operational, Strategic, Risk, Growth)
- 3-5 major insights with confidence levels
- Specific follow-up queries to drill deeper

**Use when:** You want comprehensive analysis and don't know where to start

---

### 2. 🚀 **Actionable Strategy**
**Best for:** Creating prioritized action plans  
**Time required:** 3-5 minutes  
**What you get:**
- WSJF-prioritized actions (Business Value / Effort)
- 3-phase implementation plan (0-30, 30-90, 90+ days)
- Clear ownership, timelines, and success metrics

**Use when:** You need to turn insights into action steps

---

### 3. 📊 **Historical Comparison**
**Best for:** Understanding trends and projecting future performance  
**Time required:** 2-3 minutes  
**What you get:**
- YoY, MoM, QoQ, STLY trend analysis
- Trend classification (Structural, Cyclical, Seasonal, Random)
- 3-scenario projections (Optimistic, Base, Pessimistic)

**Use when:** You want to understand how performance is changing over time

---

### 4. 💡 **Key Insights & Executive Summary**
**Best for:** Creating executive summaries for stakeholders  
**Time required:** 1-2 minutes  
**What you get:**
- Top 5 insights ranked by business impact
- "Where We Are / How We Got Here / Where We're Heading" narrative
- 3-5 concrete recommendations

**Use when:** You need to present findings to executives or stakeholders

---

### 5. ⚡ **Next Action Steps (Tactical)**
**Best for:** Immediate 7-30 day action planning  
**Time required:** 1-2 minutes  
**What you get:**
- Actions categorized by: Collections, Efficiency, Revenue, Cost, Risk
- Time-sequenced: This Week, Next Two Weeks, This Month
- Specific tasks with "What, Who, When, How, Why, Measure"

**Use when:** You need quick wins and tactical improvements

---

### 6. 🎯 **Data Explanation & Context**
**Best for:** Understanding what the data means (for non-financial audiences)  
**Time required:** 1-2 minutes  
**What you get:**
- Plain-language metric definitions
- Business context for the numbers
- Guidance on what questions to ask next

**Use when:** You need to explain financial data to non-financial team members

## 📊 Dashboard-Specific Prompts

Each dashboard also has 9-12 quick prompts tailored to its data:

### Business Comparison Dashboard
- **Growth & Trends:** YoY growth, market share, declining businesses
- **Financial Health:** Collection days, average order value, cash flow
- **Recommendations:** Resource allocation, marketing budget, intervention needs

### Consolidated Revenue Dashboard
- **Cash Flow Analysis:** Aging buckets, collection risks, cash flow projections
- **Revenue Trends:** Business contributions, monthly patterns, seasonal effects
- **Risk Assessment:** Outstanding invoices, bad debt risk, collection priorities

### Seasonality Dashboard
- **Seasonal Patterns:** Peak months, slow periods, 3-year averages
- **Marketing Strategy:** Campaign timing, inventory planning, promotional support
- **Forecasting:** Q1 projections, holiday season, budget allocation

### Forecast Dashboard
- **Scenario Analysis:** Budget scenarios, risk assessment, confidence levels
- **Risk Factors:** External threats, controllable vs. uncontrollable risks
- **Business Planning:** Quarterly targets, expense budgets, investment opportunities

## ✨ Custom Prompts

Don't see the question you want? Use the **Custom Prompt** option at the bottom of the dropdown:

1. Click "Custom Prompt"
2. Type your question in the text box
3. Click "Export with Custom Prompt"
4. Paste into AI chat

**Tip:** Be specific! Instead of "analyze this", try "What are the top 3 risks in this forecast and how should I mitigate them?"

## 💡 Pro Tips

### Tip 1: Use Strategic Prompts First
Start with a strategic prompt (like Deep Insights Analysis) to get a comprehensive view, then follow up with dashboard-specific prompts to drill into details.

### Tip 2: Chain Prompts
After getting results from one prompt, you can paste another prompt to dig deeper. The AI will have context from the previous analysis.

### Tip 3: Compare Dashboards
Export data from multiple dashboards and ask the AI to compare findings:
- Export Business Comparison data → Paste into AI
- Export Seasonality data → Paste into same chat
- Ask: "How do seasonal patterns explain the business performance differences?"

### Tip 4: Save Favorite Insights
Keep a document with insights you get from each analysis. Over time, you'll build a knowledge base of your business patterns.

### Tip 5: Use Follow-Up Suggestions
Strategic prompts include "Follow-Up Actions" sections where the AI suggests additional analyses. Use these to discover new insights!

## ❓ FAQ

### Q: Can I edit a prompt before sending?
**A:** Yes! After clicking a prompt, the text is copied to your clipboard. You can paste it into a text editor, modify it, then paste into your AI chat.

### Q: Do strategic prompts work with all dashboards?
**A:** Yes! Strategic prompts are designed to work with any Xero dashboard data. The AI will adapt its analysis to whatever data you provide.

### Q: Can I use prompts with non-Xero data?
**A:** The prompts are optimized for Xero financial data, but the strategic frameworks (like WSJF prioritization and scenario planning) can work with any business data. Just export your data and paste it along with the prompt.

### Q: How do I know which prompt to use?
**A:** 
- **Need quick answer?** → Dashboard-specific prompts
- **Need comprehensive analysis?** → Deep Insights Analysis
- **Need action plan?** → Actionable Strategy or Next Action Steps
- **Presenting to executives?** → Key Insights & Executive Summary
- **Understanding trends?** → Historical Comparison

### Q: Can I share prompts with my team?
**A:** Yes! The prompts are just text. You can copy them and share via email, Slack, or your team wiki. Your team can paste them into their own AI chats with their dashboard data.

### Q: What AI assistants work with this?
**A:** Any AI assistant that accepts text input works:
- ChatGPT (OpenAI)
- Claude (Anthropic)
- Gemini (Google)
- Your company's AI agent system
- Any custom AI chat interface

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### Problem: "No data available" error
**Solution:** Wait for the dashboard to finish loading, then try clicking Quick Prompts again.

### Problem: Dropdown won't close
**Solution:** Click anywhere outside the dropdown, or press the Escape key.

### Problem: Clipboard copy doesn't work
**Solution:** Your browser might block clipboard access. Check your browser permissions for clipboard access, or manually copy the text.

### Problem: AI gives generic response
**Solution:** Make sure you pasted the **entire** clipboard content, including the dashboard data at the bottom. Strategic prompts include both the question framework AND your data.
