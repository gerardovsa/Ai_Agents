## Artwork Requirements

### File Specifications

- **Format:** High-resolution PDF (press-ready)
- **Color Mode:** CMYK (not RGB)
- **Resolution:** 300dpi minimum
- **Bleed:** 3mm bleed on all sides
- **Safe Zone:** 5mm inside margin for text/logos
- **Page Setup:** 297×210mm finished size + 3mm bleed = 303×216mm

### What We Need

1. **Cover (4 pages):**
   - Outside front cover (page 1)
   - Inside front cover (page 2)
   - Inside back cover (page 19)
   - Outside back cover (page 20)

2. **Internal Pages (16 pages):**
   - Pages 3-18 (2-page spreads preferred)

3. **Artwork Layout:**
   - All pages as single PDF
   - Pages in print order
   - Fonts embedded or outlined
   - Images linked or embedded
