## TECHNICAL DETAILS

### Calculation Formula
1. Calculate sheets: `(quantity / cards_per_sheet)`
   - 90x55mm size: 21 cards per sheet
   - 90x45mm size: 30 cards per sheet
2. Stock cost: `(sheets / 1000) × stock_price`
   - Satin 350GSM: $180/1000
   - King Kong: $312/1000
   - EcoStar: $220/1000
3. Print cost: `sheets × sides × print_rate`
   - Colour: $0.062/sheet
   - B&W: $0.02/sheet
4. Celloglaze cost: `(sheets × celloglaze_rate) + setup`
   - 1-side: $0.186/sheet + $16 setup
   - 2-side: $0.372/sheet + $32 setup
   - SILK FEEL 1-side: $0.23/sheet
   - SILK FEEL 2-side: $0.46/sheet
5. Setup costs: `$15 (imposition) + $15 (guillotine)`
6. Artwork costs: `(artworks - 1) × $15` (first free)
7. BizCost: Sum all direct costs
8. Apply profit margin tier:
   - **NO celloglaze:** Higher 13-tier system (50-90%)
   - **WITH celloglaze:** Lower 11-tier system (40-80%)
9. Subtotal: BizCost + Profit
10. **SPECIAL: DOUBLE GST APPLICATION**
    - First GST: `subtotal × 1.1`
    - Second GST: `result × 1.1`
    - Effective tax: **21% total (×1.21)**
