#  Required Render Environment Variables for v10

Copy these to Render Dashboard  Your v10 Service  Environment:

GOOGLE_REDIRECT_URI=https://ai-agents-v10.onrender.com/api/auth/google/callback
MICROSOFT_REDIRECT_URI=https://ai-agents-v10.onrender.com/api/auth/microsoft/callback
GOOGLE_OAUTH_REDIRECT_URI=https://ai-agents-v10.onrender.com/oauth2callback
