## 📋 The 6 Strategic Prompts

### 1. 🔬 **Deep Insights Analysis**
**Purpose:** Extract maximum insights using multi-domain analysis  
**Framework:** 4-step structured approach

**Steps:**
1. **Multi-Perspective Analysis** - Analyze from 5 business perspectives:
   - Financial health (cash flow, profitability, collection efficiency)
   - Operational performance (transaction volumes, processing times)
   - Strategic positioning (market trends, competitive dynamics)
   - Risk assessment (volatility, exposure, concentration)
   - Growth trajectory (momentum, acceleration, sustainability)

2. **Domain Identification & Deep Dive** - Find 3-5 major patterns, perform statistical analyses

3. **Insight Explanation & Reasoning** - For each insight:
   - Clear statement
   - Supporting data patterns
   - Business implications
   - Confidence level (High/Medium/Low)
   - Caveats and limitations

4. **Follow-Up Actions** - Suggest additional SQL queries, comparative analyses, predictive models

**Output:** Comprehensive insight report with executive summary, detailed findings, and actionable next steps

---

### 2. 🚀 **Actionable Strategy Development**
**Purpose:** Develop prioritized action plan that maximizes business performance  
**Framework:** 5-step with WSJF prioritization

**Steps:**
1. **Opportunity & Challenge Identification** - Identify high-impact opportunities, critical challenges, hidden patterns

2. **WSJF Prioritization** - Calculate for each item:
   - Business Value (1-10)
   - Time Criticality (1-10)
   - Risk Reduction (1-10)
   - Implementation Effort (1-10)
   - **WSJF Score = (BV + TC + RR) / Effort**
   - Rank by WSJF (highest priority first)

3. **Analysis & Reasoning** - For top 5 items, explain strategic rationale, expected outcomes, dependencies, risks, timeline

4. **Step-by-Step Implementation Plan**:
   - **Phase 1 (0-30 days):** Immediate actions, quick wins
   - **Phase 2 (30-90 days):** Strategic initiatives, capacity building
   - **Phase 3 (90+ days):** Long-term improvements, optimization

5. **Follow-Up Tool Recommendations** - Monitor progress, measure ROI

**Output:** Prioritized action plan with WSJF scores, implementation roadmap, and monitoring framework

---

### 3. 📊 **Historical Comparison Analysis**
**Purpose:** Comprehensive temporal analysis to identify trends and patterns  
**Framework:** 5-step temporal analysis

**Steps:**
1. **Temporal Analysis Dimensions** - Analyze:
   - Year-over-Year (YoY)
   - Month-over-Month (MoM)
   - Quarter-over-Quarter (QoQ)
   - Same Time Last Year (STLY)
   - Multi-Year Trends (3-5 years)
   - Seasonal Adjustments

2. **Trend Identification & Classification**:
   - Direction: Growing, declining, stable, volatile
   - Magnitude: % change, absolute change, rate of change
   - Acceleration: Speeding up or slowing down?
   - Cyclicality: Repeating patterns
   - Anomalies: Unusual spikes or dips
   - **Classify as:** Structural, Cyclical, Seasonal, or Random

3. **Causal Analysis & Context** - Investigate business events, external factors, correlations, leading indicators

4. **Projection & Scenario Planning**:
   - Project forward 3-6 months
   - Develop 3 scenarios: **Optimistic, Base Case, Pessimistic**
   - Identify trigger points
   - Recommend monitoring metrics

5. **Strategic Implications** - What trends mean for strategy, resource allocation, risks

**Output:** Historical trend report with YoY/MoM/QoQ analysis, 3-scenario projections, and strategic recommendations

---

### 4. 💡 **Key Insights & Executive Summary**
**Purpose:** Distill complex data into clear, actionable insights for executives  
**Framework:** 5-step synthesis

**Steps:**
1. **Data Comprehension** - Understand metrics, time periods, data quality; identify 5-7 most important metrics

2. **Pattern Recognition** - Look for:
   - Standout numbers (exceptional performance or concerns)
   - Surprising findings
   - Hidden relationships (correlations)
   - Missing context

3. **Key Insights Extraction (Top 5)** - For each:
   - **ONE-SENTENCE SUMMARY:** Core finding in plain language
   - **SUPPORTING DATA:** Specific numbers
   - **SO WHAT?:** Why this matters
   - **CONFIDENCE LEVEL:** High/Medium/Low
   - Rank by business impact

4. **Executive Explanation (3-part story)**:
   - **WHERE WE ARE:** Current state (2-3 sentences)
   - **HOW WE GOT HERE:** Key drivers (2-3 sentences)
   - **WHERE WE'RE HEADING:** Trajectory (2-3 sentences)

5. **Recommendations Summary** - 3-5 concrete recommendations with expected impact, effort, priority

**Output:** Executive summary with top 5 insights, data story narrative, and prioritized recommendations

---

### 5. ⚡ **Next Action Steps (Tactical)**
**Purpose:** Identify immediate tactical actions executable in 7-30 days  
**Framework:** 5-step tactical planning

**Steps:**
1. **Quick Wins Identification** - Find:
   - **HIGH IMPACT:** Meaningful improvement
   - **LOW EFFORT:** Can be done quickly
   - **LOW RISK:** Minimal downside

2. **Action Categorization** - Group into:
   - **COLLECTIONS:** Improve cash flow
   - **EFFICIENCY:** Process improvements
   - **REVENUE:** Quick revenue opportunities
   - **COST:** Immediate cost reductions
   - **RISK:** Urgent risk mitigation

3. **Action Specification** - For each:
   - **WHAT:** Specific task
   - **WHO:** Role or person
   - **WHEN:** Deadline
   - **HOW:** Method or approach
   - **WHY:** Expected outcome
   - **MEASURE:** Success metric

4. **Priority Sequence**:
   - **THIS WEEK (Next 7 days):** 2-3 urgent actions
   - **NEXT TWO WEEKS (8-14 days):** 3-5 important actions
   - **THIS MONTH (15-30 days):** 5-7 valuable actions

5. **Follow-Up Analysis** - Monitor progress and measure impact

**Output:** Tactical action plan with prioritized sequence, clear ownership, and success metrics

---

### 6. 🎯 **Data Explanation & Context**
**Purpose:** Explain data in clear business language for non-technical audiences  
**Framework:** 5-step explanation

**Steps:**
1. **Data Overview** - Describe:
   - What type of financial data
   - Time period covered
   - Businesses/entities included
   - How data was collected

2. **Metric Definitions** - For each metric:
   - Define in simple terms
   - Explain calculation
   - Describe what "good" looks like
   - Note industry benchmarks
   - Use analogies or examples

3. **Current State Explanation**:
   - Headline figures (specific numbers)
   - Are these good, bad, or neutral?
   - Compare to expectations
   - Patterns or trends visible
   - Explain for non-financial audience

4. **Business Context** - Put in context:
   - What business activities do numbers represent?
   - External factors influencing results?
   - Internal decisions reflected?
   - What does this tell us about business health?

5. **Next Steps Guidance** - Guide user:
   - What questions should they ask?
   - What additional analysis would help?
   - Who should see this data?
   - What decisions can be informed?

**Output:** Plain-language explanation with context, definitions, and guidance for next steps

## 💡 Why These Are Powerful

### 1. **Multi-Step Frameworks** = Comprehensive Analysis
Instead of a single question, AI follows structured methodology:
- Deep Insights: 4 steps (Multi-Perspective → Domain Deep Dive → Insight Explanation → Follow-Up)
- Actionable Strategy: 5 steps (Identify → WSJF Prioritize → Analyze → Plan → Monitor)
- Historical Comparison: 5 steps (Temporal Analysis → Trend Classification → Causal Analysis → Projection → Implications)

### 2. **Proven Business Methodologies**
- **WSJF (Weighted Shortest Job First)** - Industry-standard prioritization
- **3-Scenario Planning** (Optimistic/Base/Pessimistic) - Risk management best practice
- **Executive Summary Framework** (Where We Are / How We Got Here / Where We're Heading) - McKinsey-style storytelling
- **Multi-Domain Analysis** - Holistic view across financial, operational, strategic, risk, growth dimensions

### 3. **Actionable Outputs**
Every prompt delivers specific, usable outputs:
- WSJF scores with rankings
- Phased implementation plans (0-30, 30-90, 90+ days)
- 3-scenario projections with trigger points
- Top 5 insights ranked by business impact
- Time-sequenced tactical actions (This Week, Next Two Weeks, This Month)

### 4. **Confidence & Context**
Prompts instruct AI to:
- Rate confidence levels (High/Medium/Low)
- Explain reasoning behind every insight
- Identify caveats and limitations
- Provide supporting data for every claim
- Suggest follow-up analyses to validate findings

**OLD WAY (Basic Export):**
1. Click "Export for AI"
2. Paste into AI chat
3. Type: "analyze this data" ← Too vague!

**NEW WAY (Strategic Prompt):**

2. See **🔬 Deep Insights Analysis** with "STRATEGIC" badge

4. Dashboard data + full multi-step framework copied to clipboard

6. AI immediately follows 4-step framework:
   - Step 1: Multi-perspective analysis across 5 domains
   - Step 2: Identify 3-5 major patterns, perform statistical analyses
   - Step 3: Explain each insight with confidence levels
   - Step 4: Suggest follow-up SQL queries and analyses
7. **Total time: 2-3 minutes, expert-level quality**

1. **Defined once** - Added to `strategicPrompts` array at top of `getQuickPromptsForDashboard()`
