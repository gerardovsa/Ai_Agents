## 4. Sales Playbook

### 4.1 Discovery Call Script (45 Minutes)

**Objective:** Qualify prospect, identify their top 3 pain points, position VetAI as the solution

**Pre-Call Research (5 minutes):**
- Google practice website (how many vets? what's their messaging?)
- Check online reviews (what do clients complain about? "Wait times," "hard to reach by phone"?)
- Look up their PIMS (Cornerstone, ezyVet, Avimark?) on their job postings

---

**Opening (3 minutes):**

> *"Hi Dr. [Name], thanks for taking the time. I'm [Your Name] from VetAI. I see you're a [2-doctor/4-doctor] practice in [City]—congrats on [something specific from their website, like 'your 15-year anniversary' or 'winning Best Vet 2024']. Before I tell you about VetAI, I'd love to understand your operation. Can you walk me through a typical day—from when you arrive at 7am to when you leave?"*

**Why this works:**  
- Personalized (you did research)
- Consultant posture (you're here to understand, not pitch)
- Open-ended question (they talk, you learn)

---

**Discovery Questions (20 minutes):**

**1. Staffing & Workload:**
- *"How many appointments do you typically see per day per vet?"* (Looking for: 15-25 = normal, 25+ = overwhelmed)
- *"What time do your vets usually finish their medical records?"* (Looking for: "6-8pm" = SOAP note burden)
- *"How often do staff members call out sick?"* (Looking for: "Once a week" = understaffed)
- *"What's your current staff turnover rate?"* (Looking for: 30%+ = burnout issue)

**2. Administrative Burden:**
- *"How do you currently handle appointment reminders?"* (Looking for: "Receptionist calls everyone" = manual work)
- *"What percentage of appointments no-show or cancel last minute?"* (Looking for: 15-20% = lost revenue)
- *"How long does it take to generate an estimate for a client?"* (Looking for: "10-20 minutes" = slow)
- *"How do you handle after-hours client questions?"* (Looking for: "Vet answers phone/texts" = no boundaries)

**3. Systems & Technology:**
- *"What PIMS are you using?"* (Cornerstone, ezyVet, Avimark, etc.)
- *"Are you happy with it, or are there things that frustrate you?"* (Looking for: "It's clunky," "Slow," "Doesn't do X")
- *"How many other software systems do you use?"* (Looking for: 8-12 = tool sprawl)
- *"Do those systems talk to each other, or are you manually copying data?"* (Looking for: manual data entry)

**4. Financial & Business Metrics:**
- *"How do you currently track revenue by service type?"* (Looking for: "Excel" or "I don't" = no analytics)
- *"Do you know which services are profitable vs. money-losers?"* (Looking for: "Not really" = flying blind)
- *"How much do you spend annually on software subscriptions?"* (Looking for: $12K-$20K = expensive)

**5. Pain Point Prioritization:**
- *"If I could wave a magic wand and fix ONE thing about running your practice, what would it be?"* (Looking for: their #1 pain point)
- *"What keeps you up at night about the business side of your practice?"* (Looking for: financial, staffing, or operational anxiety)
