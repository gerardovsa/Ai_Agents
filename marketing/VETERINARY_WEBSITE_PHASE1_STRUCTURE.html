Frequently Asked Questions

Will this work with our PIMS?

Yes. We integrate with Cornerstone, ezyVet, Avimark, AVImark, IntraVet, ImproMed, and 15+ other systems. If you use a rare PIMS, we build custom integration (one-time $1,500 fee).

How long does implementation take?

48 hours. We do it over a weekend to avoid disrupting your week. Monday morning, your team arrives to reminders already sent and phones already being answered by AI.

What if our staff doesn't like it?

In 95% of cases, staff love it because it eliminates work they hate (manual calls, data entry). We offer a 30-day trial so you can test with your team risk-free.

Is AI safe for medical decisions?

AI assists, vets approve. For example, AI suggests a drug dose based on patient weight—but the vet reviews and approves before prescribing. AI is a safety net, not a replacement for clinical judgment.

What if our internet goes down?

Critical functions (voice-to-text SOAP notes) work offline on mobile devices and sync when internet returns. For phone answering, AI runs on cloud servers, so it keeps working even if your clinic loses internet.

How much training is required?

1-hour training session for all staff. Most features are invisible to staff (reminders send automatically, phones get answered automatically). Vets need 15 minutes to learn voice dictation—intuitive like talking to Siri.
